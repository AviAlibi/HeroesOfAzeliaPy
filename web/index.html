    A FIELD GUIDE

    How to play

    Reduce your opponent from 25 health to zero. Select cards in your hand to read them; playable cards have a glowing border.

    01 / Grow your domain

    Play one terrain each turn. Each provides one mana per turn. Its play cost is its value minus one. Multi-domain terrain provides its types immediately, but no mana on the turn it enters.

    02 / Build your army

    Pay the shown mana cost and control all required domains. Creatures usually wait until your next turn to attack. Haste lets them attack immediately. You may control eight creatures.

    03 / Choose your battles

    Select one of your creatures, then a highlighted enemy or the opponent's portrait. Attacks deal simultaneous retaliation damage. Reachable Guards must be attacked first. Each creature attacks once per turn.

    04 / Turn the tide

    Spells resolve once. Enchants attach lasting buffs or debuffs to a creature. Select a targeted card, then click a highlighted creature. Inspect creatures to see durations and attached enchants.

    Domains and counterplay

    Flying defenders require Flying attackers. Water Breathing defenders require Water Breathing attackers. Ward prevents selected enemy spells and enchants, not combat or area effects. Grounded suppresses Flying; water effects extinguish Burning; Regeneration counters Poisoned.

    Losing your last sea terrain permanently removes Water Breathing. Restoring sea does not restore the buff; apply it again. The same applies to other terrain-dependent statuses.

    Timing, healing, and enchantments

    Timed statuses count down at the affected player's turn end. Enchants override those durations and last until removed or countered. Identical statuses replace rather than stack. Damage persists; healing cannot exceed maximum health. Losing bonus health can kill a damaged creature. Empty decks cause one fatigue damage per attempted draw.

    Local table and controls

    Click a card to inspect it, then use its action or choose a highlighted target. Cancel selection with Escape. The inspector lets you sacrifice your own terrain or dismiss an enchant you control. Those choices can remove buffs and kill creatures. Click a graveyard count to inspect used cards.

    This is one local table shared by browser tabs. Refreshing reconnects to the current duel; closing or restarting the Python server clears it. Hotseat is for players sharing one screen, not secure remote multiplayer. AI uses the public board, not your hidden hand.
